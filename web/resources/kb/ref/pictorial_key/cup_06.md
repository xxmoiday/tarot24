# cup_06 — Six of Cups
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktcu06.htm

## Description
"Children in an old garden, their cups filled with flowers."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "A card of the past and of memories, looking back, as--for example--on childhood; happiness, enjoyment, but coming rather from the past; things that have vanished." An alternative interpretation notes: "new relations, new knowledge, new environment, and then the children are disporting in an unfamiliar precinct."
Reversed: "The future, renewal, that which will come to pass presently."
