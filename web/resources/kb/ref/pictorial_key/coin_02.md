# coin_02 — Two of Pentacles
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktpe02.htm

## Description
"A young man, in the act of dancing, has a pentacle in either hand, and they are joined by that endless cord which is like the number 8 reversed."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "On the one hand it is represented as a card of gaiety, recreation and its connexions, which is the subject of the design; but it is read also as news and messages in writing, as obstacles, agitation, trouble, embroilment."
Reversed: "Enforced gaiety, simulated enjoyment, literal sense, handwriting, composition, letters of exchange."
