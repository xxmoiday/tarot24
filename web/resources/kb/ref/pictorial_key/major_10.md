# major_10 — Wheel of Fortune
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktar10.htm

## Description
"The symbolic picture stands for the perpetual motion of a fluidic universe and for the flux of human life. The Sphinx is the equilibrium therein."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "Behind the general notion expressed in the symbol there lies the denial of chance and the fatality which is implied therein."
Reversed: No reversed meaning is given on this page of the source text.
