## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "It represents also the perfection and end of the Cosmos, the secret which is within it, the rapture of the universe when it understands itself in God." "It is further the state of the soul in the consciousness of Divine Vision, reflected from the self-knowing spirit." "It is...the state of the restored world when the law of manifestation shall have been carried to the highest degree of natural perfection."
