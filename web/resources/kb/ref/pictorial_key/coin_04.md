# coin_04 — Four of Pentacles
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktpe04.htm

## Description
"A crowned figure, having a pentacle over his crown, clasps another with hands and arms; two pentacles are under his feet. He holds to that which he has."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "The surety of possessions, cleaving to that which one has, gift, legacy, inheritance."
Reversed: "Suspense, delay, opposition."
