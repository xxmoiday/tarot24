# major_19 — The Sun
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktar19.htm

## Description
The card depicts "the naked child mounted on a white horse and displaying a red standard," representing "the great and holy light which goes before the endless procession of humanity, coming out from the walled garden of the sensitive life."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "The card signifies, therefore, the transit from the manifest light of this world, represented by the glorious sun of earth, to the light of the world to come, which goes before aspiration and is typified by the heart of a child." "The sun is that of consciousness in the spirit--the direct as the antithesis of the reflected light," and the child represents "simplicity and innocence in the sense of wisdom" and "the restored world."
Reversed: No reversed meaning is given on this page of the source text.
