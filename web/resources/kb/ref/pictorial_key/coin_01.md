# coin_01 — Ace of Pentacles
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktpeac.htm

## Description
"A hand--issuing, as usual, from a cloud--holds up a pentacle."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "Perfect contentment, felicity, ecstasy; also speedy intelligence; gold."
Reversed: "The evil side of wealth, bad intelligence; also great riches." Waite adds: "In any case it shews prosperity, comfortable material conditions, but whether these are of advantage to the possessor will depend on whether the card is reversed or not."
