# major_08 — Strength
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktar08.htm

## Description
"A woman, over whose head there broods the same symbol of life which we have seen in the card of the Magician, is closing the jaws of a lion."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "Fortitude, in one of its most exalted aspects, is connected with the Divine Mystery of Union." "The card has nothing to do with self-confidence in the ordinary sense...but it concerns the confidence of those whose strength is God."
Reversed: No reversed meaning is given on this page of the source text.
