# major_04 — The Emperor
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktar04.htm

## Description
"He has a form of the Crux ansata for his sceptre and a globe in his left hand. He is a crowned monarch--commanding, stately, seated on a throne, the arms of which are fronted by rams' heads."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "He is executive and realization, the power of this world, here clothed with the highest of its natural attributes." "They signify also--and the male figure especially--the higher kingship, occupying the intellectual throne."
Reversed: No reversed meaning is given on this page of the source text.
