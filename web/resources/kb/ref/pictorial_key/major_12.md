# major_12 — The Hanged Man
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktar12.htm

## Description
"The gallows from which he is suspended forms a Tau cross, while the figure--from the position of the legs--forms a fylfot cross. There is a nimbus about the head of the seeming martyr." The text further notes that "the tree of sacrifice is living wood, with leaves thereon; that the face expresses deep entrancement, not suffering; that the figure, as a whole, suggests life in suspension, but life and not death."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "It expresses the relation, in one of its aspects, between the Divine and the Universe." "He who can understand that the story of his higher nature is imbedded in this symbolism will receive intimations concerning a great awakening that is possible, and will know that after the sacred Mystery of Death there is a glorious Mystery of Resurrection."
