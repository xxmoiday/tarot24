# major_13 — Death
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktar13.htm

## Description
"The mysterious horseman moves slowly, bearing a black banner emblazoned with the Mystic Rose, which signifies life. Between two pillars on the verge of the horizon there shines the sun of immortality."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "The veil or mask of life is perpetuated in change, transformation and passage from lower to higher, and this is more fitly represented in the rectified Tarot by one of the apocalyptic visions than by the crude notion of the reaping skeleton," signifying "rebirth, creation, destination, renewal, and the rest."
Reversed: No reversed meaning is given on this page of the source text.
