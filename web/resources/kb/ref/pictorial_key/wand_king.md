# wand_king — King of Wands
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktwaki.htm

## Description
The King is described as "dark, ardent, lithe, animated, impassioned, noble." He "uplifts a flowering wand" and wears "a cap of maintenance beneath his crown." A lion is "emblazoned on the back of his throne."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "Dark man, friendly, countryman, generally married, honest and conscientious." The card "always signifies honesty" and may indicate "news concerning an unexpected heritage to fall in before very long."
Reversed: "Good, but severe; austere, yet tolerant."
