# major_11 — Justice
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktar11.htm

## Description
"The figure is seated between pillars, like the High Priestess."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: The card concerns "the moral principle which deals unto every man according to his works," which is distinct from "the spiritual justice which is involved in the idea of election."
Reversed: No reversed meaning is given on this page of the source text.
