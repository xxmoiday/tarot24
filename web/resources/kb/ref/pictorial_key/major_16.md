# major_16 — The Tower
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktar16.htm

## Description
This page of the source text does not contain a separate paragraph describing the card's pictured imagery; Waite's discussion is entirely interpretive. He writes: "Occult explanations attached to this card are meagre and mostly disconcerting. It is idle to indicate that it depicts ruin in all its aspects, because it bears this evidence on the surface."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "I agree rather with Grand Orient that it is the ruin of the House of Life, when evil has prevailed therein, and above all that it is the rending of a House of Doctrine." "I understand that the reference is, however, to a House of Falsehood." "It is more correctly a question of analogy; one is concerned with the fall into the material and animal state, while the other signifies destruction on the intellectual side." "The Tower has been spoken of as the chastisement of pride and the intellect overwhelmed in the attempt to penetrate the Mystery of God; but in neither case do these explanations account for the two persons who are the living sufferers." "The one is the literal word made void and the other its false interpretation. In yet a deeper sense, it may signify also the end of a dispensation."
Reversed: No reversed meaning is given on this page of the source text.
