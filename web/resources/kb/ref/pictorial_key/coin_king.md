# coin_king — King of Pentacles
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktpeki.htm

## Description
"The figure calls for no special description; the face is rather dark, suggesting also courage, but somewhat lethargic in tendency." "The bull's head should be noted as a recurrent symbol on the throne." "The sign of this suit is represented throughout as engraved or blazoned with the pentagram, typifying the correspondence of the four elements in human nature and that by which they may be governed."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "Valour, realizing intelligence, business and normal intellectual aptitude, sometimes mathematical gifts and attainments of this kind; success in these paths."
Reversed: "Vice, weakness, ugliness, perversity, corruption, peril."
