# major_17 — The Star
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktar17.htm

## Description
"A great, radiant star of eight rays, surrounded by seven lesser stars--also of eight rays. The female figure in the foreground is entirely naked. Her left knee is on the land and her right foot upon the water. She pours Water of Life from two great ewers, irrigating sea and land."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "The summary of several tawdry explanations says that it is a card of hope. On other planes it has been certified as immortality and interior light."
Reversed: No reversed meaning is given on this page of the source text.
