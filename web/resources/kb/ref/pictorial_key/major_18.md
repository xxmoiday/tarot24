# major_18 — The Moon
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktar18.htm

## Description
"The moon is increasing on what is called the side of mercy, to the right of the observer. It has sixteen chief and sixteen secondary rays. The path between the towers is the issue into the unknown. The dog and wolf are the fears of the natural mind in the presence of that place of exit, when there is only reflected light to guide it."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "The card represents life of the imagination apart from life of the spirit." "The intellectual light is a reflection and beyond it is the unknown mystery which it cannot shew forth."
Reversed: No reversed meaning is given on this page of the source text.
