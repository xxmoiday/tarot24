# major_03 — The Empress
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktar03.htm

## Description
"A stately figure, seated, having rich vestments and royal aspect, as of a daughter of heaven and earth. Her diadem is of twelve stars, gathered in a cluster. The symbol of Venus is on the shield which rests near her. A field of corn is ripening in front of her, and beyond there is a fall of water."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "She is above all things universal fecundity and the outer sense of the Word." "The card of the Empress signifies the door or gate by which an entrance is obtained into this life, as into the Garden of Venus."
Reversed: No reversed meaning is given on this page of the source text.
