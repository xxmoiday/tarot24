# major_09 — The Hermit
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktar09.htm

## Description
"The variation from the conventional models in this card is only that the lamp is not enveloped partially in the mantle of its bearer, who blends the idea of the Ancient of Days with the Light of the World. It is a star which shines in the lantern."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "This is a card of attainment, and to extend this conception the figure is seen holding up his beacon on an eminence." "His beacon intimates that 'where I am, you also may be.'"
Reversed: No reversed meaning is given on this page of the source text.
