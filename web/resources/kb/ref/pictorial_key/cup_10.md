# cup_10 — Ten of Cups
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktcu10.htm

## Description
"Appearance of Cups in a rainbow; it is contemplated in wonder and ecstacy by a man and woman below, evidently husband and wife." The man's "right arm is about her; his left is raised upward; she raises her right arm." Additionally, "The two children dancing near them have not observed the prodigy but are happy after their own manner," and "there is a home-scene beyond."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "Contentment, repose of the entire heart; the perfection of that state; also perfection of human love and friendship." The card may also indicate "a person who is taking charge of the Querent's interests" when appearing with other picture-cards, or represent "the town, village or country inhabited by the Querent."
Reversed: "Repose of the false heart, indignation, violence."
