# cup_02 — Two of Cups
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktcu02.htm

## Description
"A youth and maiden are pledging one another, and above their cups rises the Caduceus of Hermes, between the great wings of which there appears a lion's head." Waite notes this symbol is "a variant of a sign which is found in a few old examples of this card," adding that "some curious emblematical meanings are attached to it, but they do not concern us in this place."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "Love, passion, friendship, affinity, union, concord, sympathy, the interrelation of the sexes, and--as a suggestion apart from all offices of divination--that desire which is not in Nature, but by which Nature is sanctified."
Reversed: No reversed meaning is given on this page of the source text.
