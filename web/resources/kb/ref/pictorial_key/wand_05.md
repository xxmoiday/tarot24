# wand_05 — Five of Wands
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktwa05.htm

## Description
"A posse of youths, who are brandishing staves, as if in sport or strife. It is mimic warfare."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "Imitation, as, for example, sham fight, but also the strenuous competition and struggle of the search after riches and fortune." The card also connects with "the battle of life," and certain interpretations suggest "it is a card of gold, gain, opulence."
Reversed: "Litigation, disputes, trickery, contradiction."
