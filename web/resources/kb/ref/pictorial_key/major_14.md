# major_14 — Temperance
Source: https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/pktar14.htm

## Description
"A winged angel, with the sign of the sun upon his forehead and on his breast the square and triangle of the septenary... It is held to be pouring the essences of life from chalice to chalice. It has one foot upon the earth and one upon waters, thus illustrating the nature of the essences."

## Divinatory meanings
Upright: "It is called Temperance fantastically, because, when the rule of it obtains in our consciousness, it tempers, combines and harmonises the psychic and material natures."
Reversed: No reversed meaning is given on this page of the source text.
